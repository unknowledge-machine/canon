# Paper-013 — Empirical Replication Studies: SEG26 Calibration and Cross-Validation

## Abstract

Planned replication study for empirical calibration of OEP theorem parameters. Phase 1 targets
N≥200 CEOs and N>2000 organizations with TIP/CBP instrument data. Primary outputs: calibrated ρ(N)
coefficients, α_i attenuation values for Supply Conservation Theorem (Theorem-007), and
cross-validation of MPCC (Mandatory Presence Capture Coefficient) from SEG26 (Paper-012).
