# Organizational Epistemology Failure Modes:
# A Case Compendium

## Abstract

This compendium documents empirically observed failure
modes in organizational epistemology, drawn from field
work across public sector, technology, healthcare, and
financial services organizations. Each case is analyzed
through the lens of the Organizational Epistemology
Project's formal protocols and theorems, demonstrating
how local incentive rationality produces systemic
epistemic failure. Cases include: the 5,000-person
program cancelled over a missing honorific; the 10-year
section head dismissed for lacking a doctorate; the new
director demanding specialists to teach 10-year veterans;
the 10% raise denied to a unique technical specialist
resulting in 10× external cost; and the narcissistic CEO
mandating physical presence for narcissistic supply.
Each case is mapped to the OEP canon (RFCs, Theorems,
Papers) and classified in the Epistemic Failure Mode
Taxonomy (RFC-0029).

> **Dual-Layer Notice**: This paper evaluates diagnostic
> instruments defined in the OEP RFC series. While those
> documents employ satirical framing, the empirical
> claims herein are substantiated by the cited literature
> and field observations. The OSC does not exist. The
> failures, however, do.

## 1. Introduction

Organizational epistemology — the study of how
organizations know what they know, how they forget what
they knew, and how they decide what to do with knowledge
— reveals a consistent pattern: failures are rarely
caused by ignorance. They are caused by epistemic
structures that make knowing impossible, forgetting
inevitable, and deciding irrational from a systemic
perspective while remaining perfectly rational from
each actor's local incentive frame.

The Organizational Epistemology Project (OEP) provides
a formal apparatus for diagnosing these pathologies:
RFCs specify protocols and metrics; Theorems provide
formal bounds; Papers provide empirical validation.
This compendium applies that apparatus to six
empirically observed failure modes.

Each case is presented with:
- **Narrative**: What happened (anonymized)
- **OEP Mapping**: Which RFCs, Theorems, and Papers
  explain the failure
- **Failure Mode Classification**: Per RFC-0029
- **Mechanism**: How local rationality produced
  systemic failure
- **Cost Quantification**: Where measurable
- **Prevention**: Which OEP protocols would have
  detected or prevented the failure

## 2. Case Studies

### 2.1 Case FM-001: The Honorific Cancellation

**Narrative:** A large public-sector digital transformation
program serving thousands of employees across multiple
agencies and tens of thousands of citizens was cancelled
weeks before public launch. The program had over a hundred
staff, consistent delivery metrics (velocity stable,
incidents below threshold, user satisfaction above 85%),
and all regulatory checkpoints passed. Cancellation
trigger: the announcement to thousands of affected
employees listed the program director's name without
full honorifics and omitted the doctoral title. The
director's office retracted the communication; a "review
period" was declared; the program was terminated within
30 days. External contracts were terminated at penalty;
over a hundred person-years of institutional knowledge
were lost.

**OEP Mapping:**
| RFC/Theorem | Relevance |
|-------------|-----------|
| RFC-0001 (HOP) | Executive epistemic capture: status signal > operational evidence |
| RFC-0004 (DTS) | Decision Theater: cancellation performed as governance |
| RFC-0017 (CBP) | CBP bypassed: no coffee-break synchronization with teams |
| RFC-0028 (MPEP) | NPCC=0.73: narcissistic supply drove cancellation |
| Theorem-001 (CMT) | Coffee machine network ignored; decision made in isolation |

**Failure Mode Classification (RFC-0029):**
- **FM-EC-01**: Status Signal Override — symbolic threat
  overrides operational evidence
- **FM-DT-03**: Performative Cancellation — decision
  theater with material consequences

**Mechanism:** The director's narcissistic supply
(RFC-0028) was disrupted by a missing honorific.
The director's epistemic capture (RFC-0001) meant
status signals were processed as higher-fidelity
evidence than all operational metrics combined.
The decision was made through direct executive
authority, bypassing all governance structures
(steering committee, program board, product owners),
confirming that governance structures were
performative (RFC-0004, RFC-0014).

**Cost:** ~120 person-years institutional knowledge
lost; external contract penalties; 50,000 citizens
affected; program rebuild estimated at 3× original
cost.

**Prevention:**
- RFC-0001 HOP: ECI threshold triggers mandatory
  shadowing before executive decisions on programs
  with ECI > 50
- RFC-0023 (PGP): Promotion Gate requires evidence
  review before cancellation
- RFC-0017 (CBP): Mandatory coffee-break sync with
  delivery teams before executive program decisions

### 2.2 Case FM-002: The Doctorate Deficit

**Narrative:** A senior practitioner with over a decade
of operational experience across every function of the
role — having worked in every unit, managed every
process, and led every major incident — reported to a
new director. On the director's first day, the director
expressed surprise that a senior role holder lacked a
doctorate, referring to the role as "merely a
bachelor's." The practitioner detailed their career
trajectory through every function of the role. The
director did not reciprocate the coffee-machine
acknowledgment (RFC-0017's MUG_FLASH).

**OEP Mapping:**
| RFC/Theorem | Relevance |
|-------------|-----------|
| RFC-0001 (HOP) | Epistemic capture: director's position conferred perceived epistemic authority that discounted 10+ years tacit knowledge |
| RFC-0005 (KGMF) | Gravity well: director's position created gravity well attracting external validation, repelling internal expertise |
| RFC-0010 (EIRP) | Testimonial injustice: section head's credibility discounted due to credential prejudice |
| RFC-0017 (CBP) | CHP handshake failure: MUG_FLASH not reciprocated = CHP_ABORT |
| RFC-0024 (TIP) | Trench Intelligence: director's Command Distance = ∞ |
| RFC-0027 (CBQP) | Extreme DKC: director's incompetence unrecognized due to position |
| RFC-0028 (MPEP) | Mirror-gazing: director's VDN > operational reality |

**Failure Mode Classification (RFC-0029):**
- **FM-EC-02**: Credential Epistemic Capture — formal
  credential overrides demonstrated competence
- **FM-CB-01**: CHP Handshake Failure — broken
  synchronization at hierarchical boundary

**Mechanism:** The director's position conferred
perceived epistemic authority (RFC-0001) that
immediately discounted 10+ years of tacit knowledge.
The director's narcissistic supply need (RFC-0028)
required external validation (doctorate) while
repelling internal expertise (tacit knowledge). The
failed CHP handshake (RFC-0017) at the coffee
machine was the diagnostic signal: the MUG_FLASH
was not reciprocated, signaling CHP_ABORT — the
epistemic channel was closed before it opened.

**Cost:** Section head disengagement; tacit knowledge
transfer blocked; RFC-0007 KHL acceleration for
section's domain; RFC-0009 KHI increase as section
head withholds knowledge.

**Prevention:**
- RFC-0017 CBP: Mandatory coffee-break onboarding
  for new leaders with team MUG_FLASH calibration
- RFC-0023 PGP: Promotion Gate requires tacit
  knowledge demonstration, not merely credentials
- RFC-0010 EIRP: Testimonial injustice panel for
  credential-based credibility discounting

### 2.3 Case FM-003: The "Specialists" Directive

**Narrative:** A newly appointed director, addressing
a team of senior practitioners and specialists with
over a decade in their respective roles (collective
experience exceeding a century and a half), opened
their first all-hands by announcing that "we need to
bring in specialists to teach you how to work."

**OEP Mapping:**
| RFC/Theorem | Relevance |
|-------------|-----------|
| RFC-0001 (HOP) | Epistemic capture: director's position created gravity well |
| RFC-0005 (KGMF) | Gravity well: director attracted external validation, repelled internal expertise |
| RFC-0024 (TIP) | Trench Intelligence: director's Command Distance = ∞, Trench Intelligence = 0 |
| RFC-0025 (SEP) | Succession Entropy: director blocks knowledge transfer from founders |
| RFC-0027 (CBQP) | Extreme DKC + FCI + TDI composite |
| RFC-0028 (MPEP) | MGI = ∞: mirror-gazing index maximum |

**Failure Mode Classification (RFC-0029):**
- **FM-EC-03**: Founder/Early-Staff Epistemic Erasure
  — new leader dismisses founding knowledge as
  obsolete
- **FM-TD-01**: Infinite Telepathy Demand — leader
  expects subordinates to intuit intent while
  dismissing their explicit knowledge

**Mechanism:** The director's position created a
gravity well (RFC-0005) that attracted external
validation ("specialists") while repelling internal
expertise (collective experience exceeding a
century). The director's Dunning-Kruger Coefficient
(RFC-0027) was maximal: incompetence prevented
recognition of incompetence. The Telepathy Demand
Index (TDI) was infinite: subordinates expected to
intuit the director's unstated vision while their
explicit operational knowledge was dismissed.

**Cost:** Team disengagement; RFC-0007 KHL collapse
for team's domain; RFC-0025 SEP activation —
Succession Entropy as director blocks knowledge
transfer from prior generation.

**Prevention:**
- RFC-0023 (PGP): Promotion Gate requires tacit
  knowledge demonstration
- RFC-0024 (TIP): Trench Intelligence assessment for
  new leaders — must demonstrate ground-truth
  familiarity before authority exercise
- RFC-0025 (SEP): Succession Entropy monitoring for
  new leaders in founded organizations

### 2.4 Case FM-004: The Ten-Percent Technician

**Narrative:** A senior technical specialist held
unique competency for a critical regulatory process
requiring interaction with three external regulatory
bodies, navigation of undocumented exception paths,
and interpretation of ambiguous statutory language.
No other person in the organization possessed this
tacit knowledge. The specialist requested a modest
salary adjustment to match market rate. The
compensation committee denied the request citing
"band compliance." No counter-offer, no retention
plan. Within 60 days, the specialist accepted an
external offer at seven times the previous salary.
The institution attempted replacement via external
procurement: eight months for RFP and vendor
selection, over a year for failed vendor onboarding
(the vendor lacked the specific regulatory
competency), a second RFP cycle, total cost ten
times the original specialist's annual compensation,
still incomplete at two years.

**OEP Mapping:**
| RFC/Theorem | Relevance |
|-------------|-----------|
| RFC-0005 (KGMF) | Knowledge Gravity: technician was truth vertex; committee was power vertex |
| RFC-0007 (OAPP) | KHL collapse: domain half-life collapsed from 8+ years to <6 months |
| RFC-0009 (KHIS) | KHI retrospectively high: unique competency never externalized |
| RFC-0016 (IMBP) | TEC = 8× (person who knows); actual cost = 10× |
| RFC-0014 (Consensus Theater) | Compensation committee as decision theater |
| Theorem-002 (CET) | Committee entropy: homogeneous committee evaluated heterogeneous asset |

**Failure Mode Classification (RFC-0029):**
- **FM-KH-01**: Single Point of Knowledge Failure —
  unique competency not externalized, no redundancy
- **FM-CT-02**: Compensation Committee Theater —
  generic policy applied to unique knowledge asset

**Mechanism:** The compensation committee (a
homogeneous decision body per Theorem-002) applied
generic band logic to a unique knowledge asset. The
specialist was a "truth vertex" (RFC-0005) with
high Truth Centrality; the committee was a "power
vertex" with high Power Centrality. The Knowledge
Gravity Index (KGI) was extreme: knowledge orbited
power, not truth. The 10% denial threshold was a
cognitive anchor unrelated to TEC reality. The
committee's decision entropy was high: homogeneous
signals (band compliance) evaluated a heterogeneous
knowledge asset (unique regulatory competency).

**Cost:** 10× annual compensation; 24+ months
incomplete replacement; regulatory risk during gap;
institutional reputation damage with regulators.

**Prevention:**
- RFC-0005 KGMF: KGI monitoring for gravity wells
  around unique competencies
- RFC-0007 OAPP: TEC multiplier mandates retention
  protocols for 8× roles
- RFC-0016 IMBP: TEC_aggregate monitoring triggers
  emergency backup for 8× roles
- RFC-0014/RFC-0004: Decision Theater protocols
  require heterogeneous committee composition for
  unique knowledge asset decisions

### 2.6 Case FM-006: The Mandatory Presence Theater

**Narrative:** A multi-organization study of 500 CEOs
(Matos et al., 2026) found a robust correlation
(r = 0.73, p < 0.001) between CEO narcissism (NPI-16)
and mandatory office attendance policies. The
mechanism: narcissistic leaders require physical
proximity to maintain narcissistic supply — visual
dominance, real-time compliance, admiration extraction,
and mirror-gazing opportunities that remote work
disrupts. Mandated presence is not about productivity
but narcissistic supply.

**OEP Mapping:**
| RFC/Theorem | Relevance |
|-------------|-----------|
| RFC-0001 (HOP) | NPCC inflates ECI; proximity mandates artificially reduce OR |
| RFC-0003 (EFMP) | Forced proximity creates fake alignment (low FI, high theater) |
| RFC-0005 (KGMF) | Narcissistic leaders become gravity wells (KGI ↑) |
| RFC-0017 (CBP) | CBP corrupted: coffee becomes surveillance |
| RFC-0024 (TIP) | Proximity mandates artificially reduce Command Distance |
| RFC-0025 (SEP) | Narcissistic founders block succession (no supply from heir) |
| RFC-0026 (WHP) | MGI = Brent Effect operationalized |
| RFC-0027 (CBQP) | Narcissism = extreme DKC + FCI + TDI composite |
| Theorem-001 (CMT) | Forced proximity corrupts CBP — coffee becomes surveillance |

**Failure Mode Classification (RFC-0029):**
- **FM-NP-01**: Narcissistic Proximity Control —
  mandates driven by leader's supply need, not
  productivity
- **FM-PT-01**: Presence Theater — mandated
  attendance decoupled from productivity justification

**Mechanism:** Narcissistic leaders require physical
proximity for narcissistic supply: visual dominance
(towering over subordinates), real-time compliance
monitoring, real-time admiration extraction, and
mirror-gazing opportunities (walk-throughs, town
halls, photo ops). Remote work disrupts all four
supply channels: asynchronous communication reduces
real-time admiration; autonomy reduces compliance
performances; written communication creates
accountability (reduces gaslighting); reduced
visibility reduces mirror-gazing opportunities.
Mandated presence restores the supply channels.

**Cost:** Organizations with NPCC > 0.6 show:
23% lower productivity, 34% higher turnover, 41%
lower innovation, 18% lower employee NPS, CEO tenure
34 months vs 72 months for low-NPCC CEOs.

**Prevention:**
- RFC-0028 MPEP: MPCC monitoring with board-level
  alerts at MPCC > 0.5
- RFC-0017 CBP: CBP channels must remain
  narcissism-free zones (no executive surveillance)
- RFC-0023 PGP: Promotion Gate screens for
  narcissism (NPI-16) before leadership roles
- RFC-0024 TIP: Trench Intelligence channels must
  bypass proximity mandates

### 2.7 Case FM-007: The External Appointment Cascade

**Narrative:** Following a reorganization that
created several new units, a vice-rector, a
pro-rector, and the director of the digital
transformation unit decided to hold competitions for
leaders of the newly created units. Only external
candidates were selected, chosen solely on curriculum
review and interview. The selected individuals had
no prior experience with the institution's culture,
governance, or operational realities. What emerged
was a cohort of leaders who lacked the minimum
understanding of what the institution truly was,
how decisions flowed, where the tacit knowledge
resided, or who the genuine experts were. The
selection was based solely on what the jury thought
was needed, but what revealed itself was a complete
mismatch between the appointees' mental models and
the institution's operational reality.

**OEP Mapping:**
| RFC/Theorem | Relevance |
|-------------|-----------|
| RFC-0001 (HOP) | Epistemic capture: external appointees' positions conferred perceived authority without institutional knowledge |
| RFC-0003 (EFMP) | Fragmentation: new leaders lacked operational knowledge maps; FI increased |
| RFC-0005 (KGMF) | Gravity well: external hires created new gravity wells disconnected from truth vertices |
| RFC-0007 (OAPP) | KHL acceleration: institutional knowledge not transferred to new leaders |
| RFC-0014 (Consensus Theater) | Selection committee performed "rigorous process" theater |
| RFC-0017 (CBP) | CBP channels severed: new leaders had no weak ties to operational knowledge holders |
| RFC-0023 (PGP) | Promotion Gate bypassed: no internal candidates considered, no tacit knowledge assessment |
| RFC-0024 (TIP) | Command Distance = ∞; Trench Intelligence = 0 for all appointees |
| RFC-0025 (SEP) | Succession Entropy maximized: internal succession paths severed |
| RFC-0027 (CBQP) | Selection committee's DKC + FCI: believed credentials = competence |
| RFC-0028 (MPEP) | New leaders demanded physical presence to compensate for knowledge gaps |

**Failure Mode Classification (RFC-0029):**
- **FM-EC-04**: Hierarchical Epistemic Gradient —
  epistemic authority increases with hierarchical
  distance from operations
- **FM-KL-01**: Succession Knowledge Erasure —
  leadership transition without knowledge transfer
- **FM-CT-03**: Consensus Theater — selection process
  performed legitimacy without substance

**Mechanism:** The selection committee (RFC-0014
Consensus Theater) performed a credential-based
ritual that mimicked rigor but measured only
credentialed pedigree, not institutional knowledge.
The appointees arrived with zero Trench Intelligence
(RFC-0024): Command Distance = ∞, Trench
Intelligence = 0. They immediately demanded
physical presence (RFC-0028) to compensate for
their inability to navigate the institution
remotely. Internal experts (truth vertices) were
bypassed in favor of external credentials (power
vertices), inverting RFC-0005's Knowledge Gravity.
Internal knowledge holders (RFC-0017 CBP carriers)
were excluded from the new leaders' networks,
severing weak ties.

**Cost:** Accelerated organizational amnesia (RFC-0007
KHL collapse); decision latency as new leaders
required constant briefing; RFC-0003 FI increase as
formal decisions diverged from operational reality;
turnover of internal experts who saw no succession
path (RFC-0025 SEP activation); RFC-0016 TEC
multiplier effect as multiple knowledge holders
departed simultaneously.

**Prevention:**
- RFC-0023 PGP: Mandatory internal candidate
  consideration; tacit knowledge assessment required
- RFC-0024 TIP: Trench Intelligence assessment
  mandatory for all leadership appointments
- RFC-0025 SEP: Succession Entropy monitoring;
  internal succession paths must be preserved
- RFC-0017 CBP: New leader onboarding must include
  mandatory CBP integration with operational staff
- RFC-0007 OAPP: KCP (Knowledge Continuity Plan)
  mandatory before any leadership transition

## 6. Conclusion

These five cases reveal a consistent pattern:
organizational epistemic failures are not caused by
individual malice or incompetence but by structural
incentive misalignments that make epistemic capture
rational for each actor. The director who demands
specialists to teach veterans is not stupid — they
are responding to a gravity well that rewards
external validation and punishes internal
acknowledgment. The compensation committee that
denies 10% to a unique technician is not cruel —
they are applying homogeneous band logic to a
heterogeneous knowledge asset, exactly as Theorem-002
predicts.

The OEP canon provides the diagnostic apparatus to
detect these patterns before they cascade. The
prevention framework in Section 4 operationalizes
detection and intervention. The remaining work is
institutional: embedding these protocols in
governance structures so that detection is
automatic and intervention is mandatory, not
discretionary.

The failures will continue. The question is whether
we build the instruments to see them coming.
