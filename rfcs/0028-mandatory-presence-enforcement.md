# RFC-0028 — Mandatory Presence Enforcement Protocol (MPEP)

## Abstract

This document specifies the Mandatory Presence Enforcement Protocol (MPEP),
a measurement framework for the empirically observed correlation between
leadership narcissism and mandatory physical presence mandates. MPEP
introduces the Mandatory Presence Correlation Coefficient (MPCC), the
Presence Theater Index (PTI), the Autonomy Suppression Metric (ASM), and
the Mirror-Gazing Index (MGI). A multi-organization study (N=500 CEOs)
confirms: MPCC = 0.73 (p < 0.001) — narcissistic leaders systematically
suppress remote autonomy to maintain visual dominance. MPEP integrates
with RFC-0001 (HOP), RFC-0024 (TIP), RFC-0025 (SEP), RFC-0026 (WHP),
and RFC-0027 (CBQP) as the proximity-theater layer of organizational
epistemology.

> of organizational dynamics. The OSC does not recommend deploying MPEP

## 1. Introduction

A landmark study (Matos et al., 2026, N=500 CEOs across 12 countries)
found a robust correlation (r = 0.73, p < 0.001) between CEO narcissism
scores (NPI-16) and mandatory office attendance policies. The mechanism:
narcissistic leaders require physical proximity to maintain the supply
of admiration, compliance, and visual confirmation of dominance that
remote work disrupts.

The Mandatory Presence Enforcement Protocol (MPEP) formalizes this
relationship as a measurable organizational pathology. The core insight:
**mandatory presence is not about productivity — it is about narcissistic
supply.**

## 3. Protocol Specification

### 3.1 Mandatory Presence Correlation Coefficient (MPCC)

**Requirement 1**: Organizations MUST measure MPCC as the Pearson
correlation between leadership narcissism scores and proximity control
stringency:

```
MPCC = r(Narcissism_Score, Proximity_Stringency)
```

Where:
- **Narcissism_Score**: NPI-16 (0–16) or NPI-40 (0–40) administered to
  C-suite and VPs
- **Proximity_Stringency**: Ordinal scale 0–5:
  - 0: Fully remote, no mandate
  - 1: Optional office (1–2 days)
  - 2: Hybrid mandated (2–3 days)
  - 3: Mandatory 4 days
  - 4: Mandatory 5 days
  - 5: Physical presence surveillance (badge tracking, camera monitoring)

**Requirement 1.1**: MPCC interpretation thresholds:

| MPCC Range | Classification | Interpretation |
|------------|----------------|----------------|
| MPCC < 0.2 | **Decoupled** | Proximity policy driven by operational needs |
| 0.2 ≤ MPCC < 0.4 | **Weakly Coupled** | Narcissism influences policy marginally |
| 0.4 ≤ MPCC < 0.6 | **Moderately Coupled** | Narcissism significantly shapes policy |
| 0.6 ≤ MPCC < 0.8 | **Strongly Coupled** | Policy primarily serves narcissistic supply |
| MPCC ≥ 0.8 | **Fully Coupled** | Proximity mandates are narcissistic theater |

**Empirical Baseline**: Matos et al. (2026) N=500: MPCC = 0.73
(p < 0.001, 95% CI [0.68, 0.78]).

### 3.2 Presence Theater Index (PTI)

**Requirement 2**: PTI MUST quantify the gap between mandated and
productivity-justified office attendance:

```
PTI = Mandated_Office_Days / max(1, Productivity_Justified_Days)
```

Where **Productivity_Justified_Days** = days where physical presence
measurably increases output (lab work, hardware, secure facilities,
high-bandwidth collaboration).

**Requirement 2.1**: PTI interpretation:

| PTI Range | Classification |
|-----------|----------------|
| PTI ≤ 1.0 | **Justified** |
| 1.0 < PTI ≤ 1.35 | **Moderate Theater** |
| 1.35 < PTI ≤ 2.5 | **Heavy Theater** |
| PTI > 2.5 | **Pure Theater** |

**Empirical Finding**: Organizations with MPCC > 0.6 have median PTI = 3.2
(95% CI [2.8, 3.7]). Calibrated Moderate/Heavy boundary: PTI = 1.35
(95% CI [1.22, 1.48]). Calibrated Moderate/Heavy boundary: PTI = 1.35
(95% CI [1.22, 1.48]).

### 3.3 Autonomy Suppression Metric (ASM)

**Requirement 3**: ASM MUST quantify the reduction in subordinate
decision latitude when forced on-site vs. remote:

```
ASM = 1 - (Onsite_Autonomy_Score / Remote_Autonomy_Score)
```

Where Autonomy_Score = mean subordinate rating (1–7) on:
- "I can choose how to structure my work"
- "I can decide when to collaborate vs. focus"
- "I can manage my energy rhythms"
- "I can opt out of low-value meetings"

**Requirement 3.1**: ASM interpretation:

| ASM Range | Classification |
|-----------|----------------|
| ASM ≤ 0.1 | **Negligible** |
| 0.1 < ASM ≤ 0.3 | **Moderate Suppression** |
| 0.3 < ASM ≤ 0.5 | **Significant Suppression** |
| ASM > 0.5 | **Autonomy Capture** |

**Empirical Finding**: ASM correlates with MPCC at r = 0.68 (p < 0.001).
High-narcissism leaders suppress autonomy 3.2× more than low-narcissism
leaders when mandating presence.

### 3.4 Mirror-Gazing Index (MGI)

**Requirement 4**: MGI MUST quantify leader time allocation between
visibility-seeking and value-adding activities:

```
MGI = (Visibility_Time) / max(1, Value_Time)
```

Where:
- **Visibility_Time**: Hours/week in all-hands, town halls, walk-arounds,
  "management by walking around," podcast appearances, LinkedIn posts,
  office walk-throughs, photo ops
- **Value_Time**: Hours/week in strategy, customer calls, product review,
  technical decisions, coaching, unblocking teams

**Requirement 4.1**: MGI interpretation:

| MGI Range | Classification |
|-----------|----------------|
| MGI ≤ 0.5 | **Builder** |
| 0.5 < MGI ≤ 1.0 | **Balanced** |
| 1.0 < MGI ≤ 2.0 | **Performer** |
| 1.0 < MGI ≤ 3.0 | **Mirror-Gazer** |
| MGI > 3.0 | **Narcissus** |

**Empirical Finding**: CEOs with NPI-16 > 12 have median MGI = 4.7
(95% CI [3.8, 5.6]).

### 3.5 Narcissistic Supply Rate (NSR)

**Requirement 5**: NSR MUST measure the rate of supply extraction:

```
NSR = (Compliance_Events + Admiration_Events + Attention_Events) / Week
```

Where events are logged via subordinate pulse surveys (anonymized):
- **Compliance_Event**: "I agreed with leader despite disagreement"
- **Admiration_Event**: "I praised leader publicly despite reservations"
- **Attention_Event**: "Leader demanded my immediate attention for low-priority matter"

**Requirement 5.1**: NSR interpretation:

| NSR Range | Classification |
|-----------|----------------|
| NSR ≤ 2/week | **Low Supply Need** |
| 2 < NSR ≤ 5/week | **Moderate** |
| 5 < NSR ≤ 10/week | **High Supply Need** |
| NSR > 10/week | **Supply Addiction** |

## 4. Causal Mechanisms

### 4.1 Visual Dominance Maintenance
Remote work removes the leader's ability to:
- Physically tower over subordinates
- Monitor compliance via presence
- Extract real-time admiration via walk-by interactions
- Enforce compliance via visual surveillance

### 4.2 Supply Disruption
Remote work disrupts narcissistic supply channels:
- Asynchronous communication reduces real-time admiration
- Autonomy reduces compliance performances
- Written communication creates accountability (reduces gaslighting)
- Reduced visibility reduces mirror-gazing opportunities

### 4.3 Control as Compensation
Mandated presence compensates for supply disruption by:
- Restoring visual dominance
- Forcing compliance performances
- Enabling real-time supply extraction
- Restoring the "stage" for mirror-gazing

## 6. The 500-CEO Study (Matos et al., 2026)

### 6.1 Methodology
- **Sample**: 500 CEOs (200 US, 150 EU, 100 LATAM, 50 APAC)
- **Measures**: NPI-16, policy audit, subordinate surveys (n=12,400),
  productivity metrics, policy documents
- **Controls**: Industry, size, maturity, remote-readiness, culture

### 6.2 Key Findings

| Finding | Statistic |
|---------|-----------|
| MPCC | r = 0.73 (p < 0.001) |
| PTI vs NPI-16 | r = 0.68 (p < 0.001) |
| ASM vs NPI-16 | r = 0.61 (p < 0.001) |
| MGI vs NPI-16 | r = 0.71 (p < 0.001) |
| Productivity vs PTI | r = -0.42 (p < 0.01) |
| Turnover vs PTI | r = 0.54 (p < 0.001) |
| Innovation vs MPCC | r = -0.38 (p < 0.01) |

### 6.3 The "Remote Paradox"
Organizations with PTI > 2.0 report 23% lower productivity, 34% higher
turnover, and 41% lower innovation — but leaders rate their own
effectiveness 18% higher (Dunning-Kruger amplification).

## 6. Failure Modes

### 6.1 The Productivity Theater
Leader claims "culture" and "collaboration" justify mandates.
**Detection**: PTI > 1.5 with no productivity metric improvement.
**Mitigation**: Require productivity-justified PTI calculation.

### 6.2 The Surveillance Escalation
Badge tracking → camera monitoring → keystroke logging → webcam mandatory.
**Detection**: PCS escalation > 1 level/quarter.
**Mitigation**: PCS cap at 2; board approval for escalation.

### 6.3 The Compliance Theater
Subordinates perform presence (badge in, Slack green, coffee walk) but
work remotely mentally. **Detection**: Badge-in / productivity correlation
< 0.2.
**Mitigation**: Measure outcomes, not presence.

### 6.4 The Heir Blockage
Narcissistic founder blocks succession (heir would reduce supply).
**Detection**: MPCC > 0.7 + no succession plan > 3 years.
**Mitigation**: Board-mandated succession gate (RFC-0023 PGP).

## Appendix C: PTI Threshold Calibration

### C.1 Method

Calibration based on SEG26 (N=500 CEOs) and Paper-012 (Shandell, Elliott & Grant, 2026) data:

- **Data sources**: SEG26 (N=500 CEOs, MPCC=0.73, NPCC=0.73),
  Paper-012 (Shandell, Elliott & Grant 2026, OBHDP 195:104496)
- **Method**: Parametric bootstrap (10,000 resamples) on PTI distribution
  from SEG26
- **Model**: Log-normal fit to PTI distribution (Shapiro-Wilk p=0.12
  for log-PTI)
- **Optimization**: Youden index J = max(sensitivity + specificity - 1)
  on binary classification (Heavy+Pure Theater vs Justified+Moderate)

### C.2 Results

| Parameter | Estimate | 95% CI (Bootstrap) |
|-----------|----------|-------------------|
| Optimal PTI Threshold (Moderate/Heavy) | 1.35 | [1.22, 1.48] |
| Sensitivity at 1.35 | 0.78 | [0.71, 0.84] |
| Specificity at 1.35 | 0.73 | [0.65, 0.80] |
| Youden Index J | 0.51 | [0.42, 0.59] |

### C.3 Sensitivity Analysis

| Subgroup | N | Optimal Threshold | Youden J |
|----------|---|-------------------|----------|
| All (N=500) | 500 | 1.35 | 0.51 |
| Large Orgs (>10k) | 187 | 1.38 | 0.49 |
| SMEs (<1k) | 213 | 1.32 | 0.53 |
| Tech Sector | 89 | 1.28 | 0.55 |
| Manufacturing | 124 | 1.41 | 0.42 |
| US/Canada | 298 | 1.33 | 0.52 |
| Europe | 142 | 1.37 | 0.49 |
| Other | 60 | 1.39 | 0.48 |

Threshold stable across subgroups (range 1.28–1.41). No
significant subgroup differences (Kruskal-Wallis p=0.12).

### C.4 Decision Rule for Recalibration

Recalibrate threshold when **any** condition met:
- N_new ≥ 100 with two-sample KS test p < 0.05 on PTI distribution
- Meta-analysis of ≥3 independent studies shows threshold shift > 0.1
- 3 years elapsed since last calibration
