# RFC-0037 — Goose-based Activation & Amplification Protocol (GAAP)

## Abstract

This document specifies the Goose-based Activation & Amplification Protocol (GAAP),
a Layer-A behavioral protocol for protecting Energizers (Dinamizadores) and
maximizing the propagation of technical enthusiasm in the Adaptive Space
(Paper-014; RFC-0038 iLab instantiation). GAAP prescribes three behavioral
primitives: (a) the **Escudo do Energizer (The Energizer Shield)** — prohibiting
the assignment of process management or administrative tasks to an Energizer
as a "reward" for proactivity; (b) **Autenticidade do Sinal (Genuine Signal)** —
unconditional, immediate, process-focused encouragement that celebrates productive
failure with the same intensity as success; and (c) the **Honk Transversal
(Cross-Silo Cheering)** — recognition that ignores hierarchy lines, where leaders
use the Adaptive Space brokering practice to "applaud" projects outside their
reporting lines. GAAP defines three fidelity metrics (the "Goose Score") as
private calibration lenses: EPS (Enthusiasm Propagation Speed), PNR
(Praise-to-Noise Ratio), and External Honk Factor. GAAP is voluntary by
construction: it encourages cultural norms, never mandates behavior, and its
metrics are private calibration lenses, not institutional measurements.

> **Dual-Layer Notice**: This RFC presents the Goose-based Activation &
> Amplification Protocol as a satirical vehicle for peer-reviewed findings on
> psychological safety, burnout prevention, and cross-silo knowledge flow. The
> serious layer is behavioral: protecting the catalysts of innovation from the
> bureaucratic "reward" that burns them out. The satirical layer is the
> ritualization: the protocol reads like a goose manual because, in
> bureaucracy-dominant organizations, protecting enthusiasm is treated as a
> covert wildlife conservation operation.

## 1. Introduction

### 1.1 Problem Statement: The Dilbert Effect and the Competence Tax

Organizations systematically punish their most catalytic individuals. The
**Dilbert Principle** (Adams 1996) observes that organizations systematically
promote sub-competent employees into management to limit the damage they can
do: management as containment, not reward. The corollary — the **Competence
Tax** (RFC-0035 §6.2) — is the capture of the productive by overloading: the
able are assigned more work "because they get things done" until they burn out.

In the Adaptive Space (Paper-014; RFC-0038 iLab instantiation), the **Energizer**
is the position-independent catalyst around whom projects and volunteers form.
The Energizer pushes others beyond their comfort zone, makes half-formed ideas
whole in others' hands, and sustains the enthusiasm that keeps the Adaptive
Space alive. Without explicit protection, the hierarchy "rewards" this
enthusiasm with administrative burden: "you're so good at making things happen,
now manage the process." This is the **Competence Tax** — the Energizer's
catalytic energy is converted into administrative exhaustion.

RFC-0009 (KHIS) warns that encouragement can become **Teatro de Partilha** when
the incentive structure rewards knowledge hoarding over collaboration.
Monetary incentives for "applauding" can destroy intrinsic motivation. RFC-0014
(Consensus Theater) shows that performative agreement replaces genuine
collaboration when the structure rewards the appearance over the substance.

The Energizer is the canary in the coal mine of organizational epistemic
health. When the Energizer burns out, the Adaptive Space goes silent. The
knowledge flow stops. The gravity wins.

### 1.2 Scope

GAAP is a Layer-A behavioral protocol for the Adaptive Space ecosystem. It
specifies cultural norms for Energizer protection and enthusiasm propagation,
not organizational mandates. GAAP does not create new institutional metrics,
new roles, new committees, new reporting lines, or new dashboards. Where a
metric or mechanism is referenced, it already exists (RFC-0035 ESP Energizer
archetype, RFC-0036 DOSP, RFC-0009 KHIS, RFC-0014 Consensus Theater, RFC-0017
CBP, Paper-014 Adaptive Space, RFC-0038 iLab). GAAP adds cultural norms; it
does not amend the protocols it references.

## 3. Protocol Specification

GAAP consists of three behavioral primitives. Compliance is cultural, not
mandated; the Adaptive Space ecosystem self-regulates through Psychological
Safety (RFC-0017 §3.9.4 Minimum Trust Law).

### 3.1 Escudo do Energizer (The Energizer Shield)

**Norm**: It is PROHIBITED to assign process management, administrative tasks,
or bureaucratic coordination to an Energizer as a "reward" for their
proactivity. The Energizer's role is to catalyze movement in the network, not
to manage the bureaucracy that stagnates it.

**Rationale**: The Competence Tax (RFC-0035 §6.2) is the primary capture
mechanism for Energizers. The hierarchy sees proactivity and "rewards" it with
administrative burden — "you're so good at making things happen, now manage the
Jira board." This converts catalytic energy into administrative exhaustion. The
Energizer's catalytic energy is exhausted by the very bureaucracy they
catalyzed movement around.

**Rule GAAP-1**: The Escudo do Energizer is a norm of the Adaptive Space, not a
formal policy. Violation = violation of Psychological Safety (RFC-0017 §3.9.4
Minimum Trust Law). Any participant in the Adaptive Space may invoke the
Escudo when an Energizer is assigned administrative work as "reward."

**Rule GAAP-2**: The Energizer's catalytic role is distinct from process
management. The Energizer catalyzes movement; the process is managed by those
whose role is process. Conflating the two destroys the catalyst.

**Rule GAAP-3**: The Escudo applies to all forms of "reward by burden":
meeting facilitation assigned because "you're good at it," documentation
ownership because "you know it best," compliance tracking because "you're
thorough." These are Competence Tax in disguise.

### 3.2 Autenticidade do Sinal (Genuine Signal)

**Norm**: Encouragement must be unconditional, immediate, and process-focused.
In the Adaptive Space, this means celebrating the "productive failure" of a
prototype with the same intensity as a success, preserving Psychological Safety
(RFC-0017, Paper-002).

**Rationale**: Psychological Safety (Edmondson 1999; RFC-0017, Paper-002) is not
a nice-to-have — it is a structural prerequisite for epistemic health. When
only success is celebrated, the signal becomes performative: people hide
failures, prototypes are staged, the "Honk" becomes theater. Authentic signal
celebrates the *process* — the productive failure that teaches, the half-formed
idea that becomes whole in others' hands, the question that opens a new path.

**Rule GAAP-4**: Encouragement SHALL be unconditional and immediate. It SHALL
NOT be scheduled, automated, or tied to outcome metrics. A "Great job on the
prototype, even though it failed — here's what we learned" is GAAP-compliant.
A "Great job on the successful launch" only is not.

**Rule GAAP-5**: The "productive failure" of a prototype SHALL be celebrated
with the same intensity as a success. The Adaptive Space treats failure as
data; the organization that only celebrates success gets only staged successes.

**Rule GAAP-6**: Autenticidade do Sinal applies to all participants in the
Adaptive Space, not only leaders. Peer-to-peer Honk is the strongest signal.

### 3.3 Honk Transversal (Cross-Silo Cheering)

**Norm**: Recognition must ignore hierarchy lines. Leaders use the Adaptive
Space brokering practice (RFC-0038 §4.4) to "applaud" projects of teams that
do not report to them, reducing Knowledge Gravity (RFC-0005) and increasing
Network Connectivity.

**Rationale**: Knowledge Gravity (RFC-0005) pulls recognition toward power:
leaders praise their own reports; silos applaud themselves. The Honk Transversal
inverts this gravity. When a leader "honks" for a project three levels down in
a different silo, the signal travels cross-gravity. The Adaptive Space brokering
practice (RFC-0038 §4.4.2 — the post-it wall, the Coffee Raven marginalia, the
physical presence) is the channel; no formal platform is used (RFC-0038 §4.4.1).

**Rule GAAP-7**: The Honk Transversal operates through the Adaptive Space
brokering practice (RFC-0038 §4.4.2), not through a formal platform. A leader
walking past the post-it wall and writing "This solves the cache issue we've
had for months — honk!" on a neighbor's card is GAAP-compliant. A Slack bot
that auto-posts "Great job!" is not.

**Rule GAAP-8**: The Honk Transversal must ignore reporting lines. A leader
honking for a project outside their reporting tree is the signal. Honking only
within one's own tree is gravity, not amplification.

**Rule GAAP-9**: The Honk must be specific and evidence-referenced. "Honk for
the cache fix" > "Honk for the team." Specificity = authenticity (Rule GAAP-4).

## 4. Métricas de Fidelidade (O "Goose Score")

GAAP defines three fidelity metrics — the "Goose Score" — as **private
calibration lenses** for GAAP operators (leaders, facilitators, Energizers,
facilitators of the Adaptive Space). These are **not institutional metrics**.
They are not published, dashboarded, reported, or institutionalized outside the
GAAP operating circle. The absence of infrastructure is a feature (RFC-0035
§6.3; RFC-0036 Rule DOSP-11).

**Rule GAAP-X**: EPS, PNR, and External Honk Factor are fidelity calibration
lenses for GAAP operators. They are not published, dashboarded, reported, or
institutionalized outside the GAAP operating circle. The absence of
infrastructure is a feature.

### 4.1 EPS — Enthusiasm Propagation Speed (Índice de Propagação de Entusiasmo)

**Definition**: Measures the speed (in interactions/days) at which a validated
idea in the Adaptive Space gains traction in departments distant from its
origin.

**Observation Method**: Track an idea from Adaptive Space validation (prototype
demo, post-it wall "done") to first adoption in a distant silo. Count
interaction hops and calendar days.

**Target**: Decreasing trend over time. Faster propagation = healthier
cross-silo connectivity.

**Interpretation**: High EPS = Adaptive Space ideas escape local gravity
quickly. Low EPS = ideas stall in silos; gravity wins.

**Rule GAAP-10**: EPS is a fidelity calibration lens for GAAP operators. It is
not published, dashboarded, reported, or institutionalized.

### 4.2 PNR — Praise-to-Noise Ratio (Rácio de Ruído de Louvor)

**Definition**: Distinguishes vague public praise (theater) from effective
collaboration in Project-Based Learning (PBL) projects.

**Observation Method**: Ratio = (Public praise mentions referencing specific
PBL collaboration) / (Total public praise mentions). High PNR = praise is
theater. Low PNR = praise is grounded in real collaboration.

**Target**: Low PNR (praise grounded in real collaboration dominates).

**Interpretation**: High PNR = Teatro de Partilha (RFC-0009) — praise is
performative, disconnected from real collaboration. Low PNR = Honk is grounded
in real PBL collaboration.

**Rule GAAP-11**: PNR is a fidelity calibration lens for GAAP operators. It is
not published, dashboarded, reported, or institutionalized.

### 4.3 External Honk Factor

**Definition**: Amount of encouragement received from students, external
partners, retirees, or community contributors — acting as an authenticity
sensor outside the internal "echo chamber."

**Observation Method**: Count of substantive encouragement interactions
(questions, offers to collaborate, prototype feedback) originating from
external un-captured nodes (Paper-014 §3.3 / RFC-0038 §4.3) per Adaptive Space
cycle.

**Target**: Increasing trend. External Honk = the signal escapes the echo
chamber.

**Interpretation**: External Honk Factor = authenticity sensor. Internal-only
honk may be theater; external honk cannot be mandated by internal politics.

**Rule GAAP-12**: External Honk Factor is a fidelity calibration lens for GAAP
operators. It is not published, dashboarded, reported, or institutionalized.

## 5. Integração Estrutural no Espaço Adaptativo

GAAP transforms the Adaptive Space into an **infrastructure of emotional
resilience**. The three primitives form a protection loop:

1. **Escudo do Energizer** prevents the Competence Tax from draining the
   catalyst.
2. **Autenticidade do Sinal** ensures the signal remains authentic — failure
   is data, not shame — preserving Psychological Safety (RFC-0017).
3. **Honk Transversal** amplifies the signal across gravity wells, connecting
   the Energizer's spark to distant silos.

Together, they create an **infrastructure of emotional resilience** where the
informal network protects its catalysts against Organizational Amnesia
(RFC-0007, RFC-0016) and hierarchical disinterest. The Energizer is no longer
the person who "gets things done and gets burned out" — the Energizer is the
catalyst the network protects, because the network knows: no Energizer, no
adaptation.

  protocol for the Adaptive Space infrastructure. Paper-014 §3.2 identifies
  Energizers as FF↑ catalysts; GAAP protects that FF contribution.

  Space. GAAP norms operate within the iLab's anti-theater properties.

GAAP supersedes no other RFC. It implements cultural norms consistent with
findings already documented in the RFCs above.

## 7. Security Considerations

### 7.1 Teatro de Partilha (FM-GA-01)

The organization adopts GAAP language but the encouragement remains performative:
public praise without PBL collaboration; PNR high, EPS low. The "Honk" becomes
Theater de Partilha (RFC-0009) — a new coat of paint on the same hoarding
structure.

**Mitigation**: PNR fidelity lens (Rule GAAP-11) directly detects this. If PNR
rises without EPS increase, the Honk is theater. The Autenticidade do Sinal
(Rule GAAP-4/5) demands celebration of productive failure — theater cannot
celebrate failure authentically.

### 7.2 Competence Tax 2.0 (FM-GA-02)

The organization respects the Escudo do Energizer (no admin tasks assigned) but
overloads the Energizer with "facilitation," "mentoring quotas," "culture
champion duties" — new forms of administrative capture disguised as cultural
roles.

**Mitigation**: Rule GAAP-3 explicitly covers "facilitation," "culture
champion," "mentoring quota" as Competence Tax in disguise. The Escudo do
Energizer (Rule GAAP-1) applies to all forms of "reward by burden." The
Minimum Trust Law (RFC-0017 §3.9.4) treats this as a safety violation.

### 7.3 Câmara de Eco (FM-GA-03)

The Honk Transversal operates only within a friendly clique; External Honk
Factor remains near zero. The "cross-silo" honk never crosses the actual
gravity well.

**Mitigation**: External Honk Factor (Rule GAAP-12) is the sensor. If External
Honk Factor remains near zero while internal honk is high, the Honk is a
chamber of echo. The Honk Transversal (Rule GAAP-7/8) explicitly requires
ignoring hierarchy lines and using Adaptive Space brokering practice — the
post-it wall, the Coffee Raven marginalia, the physical presence in distant
silos.

## 8. Operational Considerations

### 8.1 Deployment

GAAP requires no organizational infrastructure. The Adaptive Space requires:
- An existing Adaptive Space (Paper-014; RFC-0038 iLab or CBP venue)
- Participants who understand the three primitives
- Willingness to invoke the Escudo, practice Autenticidade, and honk transversally

### 8.2 Failure Modes

| Mode | Behavior | Outcome |
|------|----------|---------|
| Teatro de Partilha (FM-GA-01) | Public praise without PBL collaboration; PNR high, EPS low | Encouragement becomes theater; intrinsic motivation destroyed |
| Competence Tax 2.0 (FM-GA-02) | Energizer overloaded with "facilitation/culture duties" as "reward" | Catalytic energy drained by new administrative capture |
| Câmara de Eco (FM-GA-03) | Honk Transversal only within clique; External Honk Factor ≈ 0 | Cross-silo signal fails; gravity wins |
| Escudo Violado | Admin tasks assigned to Energizer as "reward" | Competence Tax triggers; Energizer burns out |
| Sinal Inautêntico | Only success celebrated; failure hidden | Psychological Safety collapses; prototypes staged |

### 8.3 Non-Goals

GAAP does not create new institutional metrics, new dashboards, new reports,
new roles, new committees, or new reporting lines. The absence of
infrastructure is a feature (RFC-0035 §6.3; RFC-0036 Rule DOSP-11).

**Rule GAAP-Y**: GAAP SHALL NOT be used to create institutional metrics,
dashboards, roles, committees, reporting lines, or approval committees. Any
such creation is a GAAP violation and reverts the protocol to theater.
